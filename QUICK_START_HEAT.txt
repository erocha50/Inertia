✅ SYSTEM IS LIVE & READY TO PLAY

Heat builds up when you run, cools down when you stop.
Keep moving to maintain momentum and damage output!

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE FLOW
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Moving (speed ≥ 2.0 units)
    ↓ Heat +30/sec
    ↓
Cold (0-24%)      → Warm (25-49%)    → Hot (50-74%)    → Burning (75-100%)
Damage: 1.0x      → Damage: 1.2x     → Damage: 1.5x    → Damage: 2.0x

Not Moving / Idle
    ↓ Heat -15/sec
    ↓ Heat drops back to Cold


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TO ADJUST DIFFICULTY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Open the Inspector and find the HeatManager autoload.
Edit these values:

• heat_gain_per_second: 30.0     (increase = harder)
• heat_loss_per_second: 15.0     (increase = more forgiving)
• movement_speed_threshold: 2.0  (decrease = easier)
