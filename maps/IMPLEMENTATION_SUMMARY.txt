🚀 HOW TO USE THIS TEST ROOM
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

QUICK START (5 minutes)
────────────────────────
1. Open res://maps/test_play_world.tscn
2. Press F5 to play
3. Walk through each zone briefly
4. Feel for problems
5. Identify which zone has issues
6. Stop (Escape) and read the guide for that zone

DETAILED TESTING (15-30 minutes)
──────────────────────────────────
1. Decide what to test (e.g., "Is sliding responsive?")
2. Open test_play_world.tscn
3. Edit CharacterBody3D transform to spawn in relevant zone
4. Play (F5)
5. Perform specific test (see TEST_ROOM_GUIDE.md)
6. Record behavior or take measurements
7. Stop and adjust variable in player_controller.gd
8. Replay same zone
9. Compare before/after
10. Document change with reasoning

TWEAKING WORKFLOW
────────────────
1. Identify problem: "Turning feels sluggish"
2. Find relevant zone: Zone 6 (Arc Turning)
3. Find relevant variables:
   - arc_threshold (angle to trigger arc)
   - arc_turn_rate (rotation speed limit)
   - corner_speed_bleed (speed loss per degree)
4. Make hypothesis: "Increase arc_turn_rate to 180"
5. Edit player_controller.gd, change ONE variable
6. Replay Zone 6
7. Evaluate: Better? Worse? Just right?
8. Repeat until satisfied
9. Quick sweep other zones for side effects
10. Document change

🎓 BEST PRACTICES FOR USING THIS ROOM
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. TEST ONE VARIABLE AT A TIME
   Don't change 5 things and wonder what broke. Change 1, test, evaluate.

2. USE THE ZONES IN ORDER
   Zone 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8
   Later zones depend on mechanics from earlier zones.

3. RECORD BEFORE/AFTER
   Video is your best friend. See the difference objectively.

4. DOCUMENT CHANGES
   "Changed X from Y to Z because W"
   Future you will thank present you.

5. REGRESSION TEST AFTER CHANGES
   Quick sweep all zones after each tweak.
   Catch unintended side effects early.

6. FEEL OVER NUMBERS
   If it FEELS right, it's right. Physics is about feel, not accuracy.

7. ISOLATE PROBLEMS
   If something feels wrong, find the zone. Don't tweak blindly.

8. UNDERSTAND THE MECHANIC
   Read the guide section for your zone.
   Understand the physics before tweaking.
