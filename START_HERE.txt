Your tutorial message system is ready! Here's how to use it in 30 seconds.

STEP 1: OPEN THE TUTORIAL MAP
─────────────────────────────
Open the file: res://maps/tutorial_map.tscn

STEP 2: CUSTOMIZE YOUR MESSAGE
───────────────────────────────
1. In the scene tree on the left, click: TutorialTriggers > TutorialTrigger1
2. On the right panel (Inspector), find these fields:
   
   message = "Welcome to the tutorial! Use WASD to move around."
   duration = 3.0

3. Change "message" to whatever you want!
4. (Optional) Change "duration" to how long it should show

STEP 3: PLAY AND TEST
──────────────────────
1. Press F5 (or click the Play button)
2. Move forward in the game
3. When you walk into the first trigger zone, your message appears!
4. Watch it fade in smoothly, stay for 3 seconds, then fade out

THAT'S IT! You're done! 🎉

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:

Q: How do I add a new trigger?
A: Right-click TutorialTrigger1 → Duplicate Node → Move it → Customize

Q: How do I change the colors?
A: See the file "CUSTOMIZE_COLORS.md"

Q: How do I change the font size?
A: Select MessageLabel → Change font_size in theme overrides

Q: Can I make the message longer?
A: Yes! The text automatically wraps. Keep messages under 2-3 sentences.

Q: How long should messages show?
A: Usually 3-4 seconds is good. Adjust the "duration" field.

Q: Do triggers fire multiple times?
A: No, by design each trigger only fires once per game session.
